The exact formula is:
  p083 + eligible * [
    0.384 * (mean3 TabPFN(P104 + F46) - mean3 TabPFN(P104))
    + 0.128 * (Ridge100(P104 + F46) - Ridge100(P104))
    + 0.128 * (exp(log-Ridge100(P104 + F46)) - exp(log-Ridge100(P104)))
  ]
The eligibility mask is the frozen076 failure fallback, aligned by ID. All paired heads use the same500 original training rows. TabPFN is the existing local3.5 checkpoint, eight estimators and seeds0/100/200. Linear heads use training-median imputation, StandardScaler and alpha100. No calibration is fitted on test predictions.

F46 contains continuous cell-flow, confidence, spatial and brightness statistics from the existing H300 auxiliary segmenter. It uses image-local1st/99th percentiles and the mean of four90-degree rotations. Native CPU float32 extraction was frozen before test-image access. H300's historical training ancestry is why the original400 development comparison is conditional.
