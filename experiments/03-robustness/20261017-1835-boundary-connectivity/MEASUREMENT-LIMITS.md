These are weak auxiliary instance masks, not true grain annotations. A mask instance may represent a phase inclusion or a pore rather than one physical grain. Base/TTA agreement can share the same model's systematic hallucinations. The very high withheld-view agreement is conditional on confident common interiors; it is not whole-image segmentation accuracy or independent annotation validation.

Target summary records these failures rather than dropping them based on hardness.

Boundary evidence weights rank locations within a source. Per-class normalization and equal image averaging mean a uniform reduction of every positive boundary weight in a whole source cancels; the method does not downweight every globally unreliable image. No claim is made that this eliminates weak-teacher bias.

Balanced BCE and balanced positive/negative pair sampling change class priors. Sigmoid outputs are affinity/boundary SCORES, not calibrated physical probabilities; their entropy is a score descriptor, not a validated posterior uncertainty. The28-dimensional graph signature uses fixed score thresholds and learned maps as predictors. It must not be presented as an unbiased physical grain count or phase fraction. No probability prior correction is fitted from outer labels or test data.

The32x32 affinity graph has8px spacing with central2x2 sampled interiors, while the boundary decoder is128x128. Narrow/very small structures can be poorly represented by the coarse graph. D4 target/edge mapping is exact, but geometric resolution is not equivalent to full256px segmentation. Fixed candidate pairs sample the graph rather than exhaust all pixel pairs. A sampled maximin BCE objective is not an exact reproduction of uniform all-pairs Rand optimization in the reference paper.
