## Transformation safety: training partitions only

Native256 grayscale source pixels, float operations, no generated microstructures, blur augmentation, added noise, crops, arbitrary inpainting, or test pixels. D4 geometry is retained as the common control.

Image-local analysis defines smooth interior regions using a fixed Gaussian derivative and within-image intensity/gradient quantiles. Photometric views modify a random subset of those regions with local contrast contraction and small brightness offsets; source-image boundary/pore regions are excluded. Fixed contrast strengths .03, .10, .30 are screened. Selected boundary pixels are separately blended toward their SAME-image surrounding 3x3 mean at .05, .15, .40 strength. These boundary variants are safety diagnostics, not candidates for training in this budget. Boundary invariance is deferred even if cheap tests pass: it needs stronger geometry evidence than these probes.

Each A213 and T320 partition independently screens strengths using only its own sources. A fixed, cheap image descriptor contains intensity quantiles, multiscale texture/gradient statistics and phase/edge proxies. Source-grouped3fold standardized Ridge(alpha100) predicts hardness from original versus transformed descriptors. A paired source bootstrap (2000 draws) must have 90% upper bound on RMSE harm <=.25 overall and <=.50 on noise>=12; this is a noninferiority SCREEN, not proof of label invariance. In addition, across-source descriptor correlations have median>=.98 and 10th percentile>=.90, 95th-percentile normalized descriptor drift<=.25, and strong-edge membership agreement>=.95. The weakest nonzero photometric strength (.03) is used only if it passes; the stronger strengths are information-loss stress tests and cannot be selected for better RMSE. If .03 fails, that partition uses D4 only and records the failure. If it fails for more than two of ten partitions, close the local-invariance branch before neural training. No safety decision may inspect B or V.
